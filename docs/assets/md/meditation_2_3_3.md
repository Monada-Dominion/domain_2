# 2.3.3 Multidimensional Meditation – Reflection

## The Triangle as an Independent Figure — Reflection

Use these prompts after practicing the triangle as an independent figure (2.3.1-2.3.2) to integrate insight and refine your next session.

---

### Immediate Practice Notes

- Session duration: How long did you practice, and was attention stable or fluctuating?
- Entry quality: How quickly could you move from body relaxation to symbolic concentration?
- Sequence clarity: Was your transition point -> line -> triangle smooth, fragmented, or forced?

---

### Three Centers and Three Spheres

- Which center felt strongest today: physical, emotional, or intellectual?
- Did you feel one center dominate while the others weakened?
- How did the three life spheres show up in your experience: biological/physical, social, and informational?

---

### Three Factors of the Triangle

- Point as possibility: Could you feel the point as the potential of existence, not just as an image?
- Movement as probability: Did your chosen direction feel intentional, habitual, or uncertain?
- Quality of movement: Was your movement inside the flow, at the boundary, or in the sense of emptiness?

---

### Switching Forms During Meditation

- Which form was easiest to hold: point, line, or triangle?
- Which form was hardest to hold, and what disrupted it?
- Did changing forms deepen concentration or break it?

---

### Daily-Life Integration

- In one real situation today, where did you notice your movement among physical, social, and intellectual axes?
- Did you act from immersion in experience or from distance and self-direction?
- What changed when you consciously chose the quality of movement?

---

### Troubleshooting and Adjustment

- Main difficulty: abstraction, emotional noise, body tension, or unstable visualization?
- One practical adjustment for your next session.
- Shorter and more frequent sessions.
- More time in relaxation before symbolic work.
- Longer stabilization on point before moving to line and triangle.
- Fewer symbolic transitions with deeper focus on one triangle quality.
