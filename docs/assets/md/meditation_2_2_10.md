
# 2.2.10 Multidimensional Meditation – Practice – The Triangle (Angle of Displacement)

This practice develops the transition from point → line → displaced line (angle) and lets you observe how the “quality of movement” changes as the line rotates between axes.

---

## Preparation (2–3 minutes)

- Sit comfortably.
- Relax the body (crown → face → neck → chest → abdomen → pelvis → legs → feet).
- Let the breath become steady.

If you feel rushed: do 6 calm breaths and begin.

---

## Recall the Point (2–5 minutes)

Briefly return to the earlier stages.

1. **Point as void / falling:** feel the “fall” into emptiness and the infinite divisibility of the point.
2. **Point as a filled unit:** feel the point as presence (awareness filling it).

Do not force images. Use sensation (breath, gravity, inner space) as your anchor.

---

## Establish the Line (5–10 minutes)

Now move to the line as an independent element.

1. **Imagine axes / directions.**
	- Up / down
	- Left / right
	- Toward yourself / away from yourself

2. **Choose one axis** (for example, the vertical).

3. **Begin bidirectional movement** along that axis.
	- Feel it as two opposite streams from one center, or as one line expanding in two directions.
	- Let the breath support it: inhale / exhale as a gentle “stretching” of the line.

Stay here until the sensation of “line” becomes clear enough to hold steadily.

---

## Create the Angle (Triangle) (10–15 minutes)

The triangle appears when the line is displaced: one point remains fixed while the other shifts, forming an angle.

1. **Fix the first point** (the origin / the point of realization of a possibility).
	- Let it be simple and stable.

2. **Hold the line from that point** along your chosen axis.

3. **Begin to displace the movement** (shift the “moving end” of the line).
	- Keep the origin steady.
	- Let the line rotate slightly toward another axis.
	- Notice: the moment rotation appears, a “third point” is implied—an influencing factor that changes *how* the possibility is realized.

4. **Explore three zones of the movement**:
	- Movement *inside* the axis / flow (pure quality).
	- Movement on the *boundary* (a light mixing).
	- Movement *outside* the axis / flow (clear mixing with another axis).

### A concrete example (optional)

If you choose **upward** as spiritual development, then a small displacement toward the horizontal can feel like a mixture of the spiritual and the social. Around “45 degrees” it becomes clearly “between” them.

Do not chase angles with mathematical precision. Work with qualitative changes.

---

## Personal Projection (3–5 minutes)

Overlay real meanings from your life onto the axes:

- What is **physical development** for you (downward / inward)?
- What is **social development** for you (horizontal)?
- What is **spiritual development** for you (upward)?

Then repeat the displacement and notice:

- Which direction you *want* to move in.
- Which direction you *actually* drift toward.
- What changes in feeling when you “turn” the movement.

---

## Duration

- Total: **15–30 minutes**.
- If the triangle is unclear, spend longer on “Recall the Point” and “Establish the Line,” then return to the angle.

---

## Troubleshooting

- **Too abstract:** use breath + a body axis (spine as up/down; shoulders as left/right).
- **Origin won’t stay fixed:** shorten the angle; return to a simple line for 1–2 minutes.
- **Mixing feels chaotic:** choose only one “secondary axis” to rotate toward; do not mix multiple rotations in one session.

## Extra notes on practice 

The "practical" side of this practice or the most applied part of this practice is in ability to merge abstaract shapes with real life conditions, events, states. 

While meditation with abstract shapes is important, to be able to work in life, during the process, not in the monastery or far away in the mountains it is important to learn to distinguish life spheres and conenct them with shapes. 

This closeley resembles programming. Assign meaning to variables and then work with them. Attach symbols to something real from your life. 

Returning to the poit as an independent shape we learnt there to concentrate on the presence in the moment and to fill the point with that attention, energy of the present moment. 

This is what we can see as grounding in physical world or going the downward direction. Concentrating in the body, in physical presence. It is not a bad thing if it is opposed to moving upward, which represents pure spirituality. 

Remember, we learnt movement in two directions at the line stage. And it is critical to maintain it. 

Then in this triange stage we move between different lines. Merge them, combine. And our results are different states, different qualities of meditation, awareness. 

Learn to generate them, enter them and distinguish them with more practice.
