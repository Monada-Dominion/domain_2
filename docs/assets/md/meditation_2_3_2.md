# 2.3.2 Multidimensional Meditation – Practice – The Triangle (Self-sufficient figure)

As always, relax, breathe, and release all tension in your body. Let go of all negative thoughts and emotions, anything that distracts you.

This meditation can be done in almost any position. Even when standing, you need to release all tension, leaving only the light effort needed to maintain proper body posture.

So, when you have reached this state of relaxation, composure, and freedom from intrusive states (I will record a separate meditation on how to reach this state), begin concentration.

First, go through the states of the point and the line.

Then begin shifting this line, as a variant of shifting movement.

And in the end, realize that there are three centers, three axes that create a coordinate system. A shift between chosen axes gives a triangle with one base at the center where the axes intersect, the present moment, and two other points. One will be on the axis chosen for movement in meditation, and the other the axis toward which the shift is directed. A line forms between them that, in essence, expresses your state.

In meditation, you travel between awareness of pure intellect or spirituality and, for example, the social environment; or yourself as a body and individual in a social environment; or as a body in the space of intellect, as a carrier and conductor, a creator of meaning.

The practice of the previous lesson focused on creating combinations and holding them, learning to enter these states through symbols of the universal matrix.

The physical center, emotional center, and intellectual center create three spheres of life:

- biological or physical,
- social,
- informational.

The sphere of spirit goes somewhat beyond these three and unites with something greater, but for now we will focus on these three.

The triangle, personifying the phenomenon of conscious choice, the choice of movement, and the choice of how this movement will happen, determines how possibility is realized.

Possibility is our life.

In meditation, concentrate on the point, on what it personifies: the possibility of human existence.

Your movement is your development in one quality or another.

Accordingly, this is probability, determining how and in what quality possibilities are realized.

And the third factor is movement purely in the flow, in emptiness, or on the boundary between flow and emptiness.

In other words, a point can move as possibility, as a filled possibility of something's existence. And it can also remain as an infinite unit receding into emptiness for concentration.

By choosing one movement, or even two movements at once, even if you imagine the line as a self-sufficient figure, immersion into the flow - direct awareness of a pure sphere, purely your emotions, purely your thoughts, purely your physical sensations - is one of the possible paths of realizing possibilities.

You immerse yourself in these spheres and surrender to them.

The same applies to movement through life.

You can go with the flow, and you can feel very deeply what is happening.

Or you can distance yourself from what natural life is, from natural development, and create something on your own.

At the same time, movement should not be against nature.

Develop spiritually as well: accumulate knowledge, expand your emotional sphere and your capacity to feel, strengthen your sensations, but move more independently. This is the other side of the point: not immersion into infinity, but the formation of a center.

And this choice between the two points, which happens just like the choice of movement along one axis or another, is the third point, the third factor influencing the realization of possibilities.

All this forms a triangle, the triangle of our life: between three spheres, between three centers that embody them in our body, and between how we make this movement.

For meditation, you mentally switch between forms of concentration: first the point, then the line.

The line also means movement.

And the third point is the direction of this movement, or the quality of this movement.

Switching from one to another, you eventually enter a state, a deep meditative state connected with three points, with three factors.

What you choose for them depends only on you. Follow what resonates with you more, and then observe it.
