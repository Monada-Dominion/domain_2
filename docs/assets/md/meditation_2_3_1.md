# 2.3.1 Multidimensional Meditation – Theory – The Triangle (Self-sufficient figure)

Last time, we explored the triangle as a shifted line, as an angle that appears when a line is displaced. This time, we need to enter into the triangle as something self-sufficient, made of three points that are connected to one another.

We need to remember that a point can take on several states, that it can be a symbol of infinite division, an abstract idea we cannot fully comprehend.

It can also represent something on a higher level, for example, on the level of a human being.

If we imagine our life as a sphere, then by moving farther away toward space, we see ourselves more and more as a point, and eventually we can become more aware that our whole life is contained in this point.

Then there is a line made of many points, understood as a process of movement between different states that make up this point, understood as endless movement in two directions. This, too, gives us one of the archetypal ideas for concentration.

Then we looked at the triangle as the possibility of something existing, as probability: a point, or a second point, and a factor that determines how this possibility will be realized.

I said that there are three axes that make up human life.

These are spirituality, materiality, and the social dimension.

For a person, these are three centers of life.

Physical, emotional, and intellectual.

The physical center corresponds to the physical world.

The emotional center largely forms the social environment through emotions. And the intellectual center forms our informational sphere of life, our connection to subtler layers of the organization of matter, such as the psyche and the psychic layer, the collective unconscious, culture, traditions, and archetypes.

At the same time, spirituality is not always perceived purely through intellect, but also through emotion, and sometimes through the physical. In general, these centers connect and mix.

That is why I said that if we choose upward movement but carry it out not strictly within one pure sphere, not purely within intellectual understanding or purely within emotion, then it goes somewhere to the boundary, or beyond the boundary, of that pure flow.

Today we must realize what the triangle itself is, and how we can immerse ourselves in awareness of this figure.
