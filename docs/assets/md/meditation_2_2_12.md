# 2.2.12 Multidimensional Meditation – Questions

## The Triangle (Angle of Displacement) — Deeper Questions

Use these questions to deepen the triangle practice and to turn it into experiments rather than repetition.

---

### Core Inquiry

- What is the essential difference between a **line** (movement in two directions) and a **triangle** (a line that has been displaced into an angle)?
- In your direct experience, what is the “third point”?
	- A factor?
	- A choice?
	- A constraint?
	- An environment?
	- A new quality of attention?

---

### Probability, Realization, and Choice

- If the first point is a possibility, and the second is probability (direction), how does the third point change the realization? Describe it in one concrete personal example.
- When you change the angle, do you feel like you changed the probability, or the quality of the result, or both?
- Does your attention “choose” the displacement, or does it *reveal* a displacement that was already happening?

---

### Experiments

- **Angle sweep:** Start with a clear line (0° displacement). Slowly increase displacement in small steps until the sensation qualitatively changes. Where is the threshold?
- **Axis swap:** Do one session where you treat “up” as spiritual; do another where “up” is social ambition (intentionally). Does the same angle produce a different state?
- **Fixed point test:** Keep the origin stable for 3 minutes. Then intentionally let it “move” (lose the fixed point). What changes first?
- **Boundary test:** Try to stay on the “boundary” (just slightly displaced) for 2 minutes without drifting to a stronger mix.

---

### Daily Integration

- Choose one life topic (health, relationships, work, learning). Identify:
	- possibility (point 1)
	- direction/probability (point 2)
	- factor/displacement (point 3)
	Then ask: which factor would you change to change the outcome?

- In a conversation, can you feel your movement shifting between “social” and “spiritual” motives? What is the factor that shifts it?

---

### Philosophical Prompts

- If movement along an axis can be “pure,” what does purity mean here: absence of mixing, or clarity of intention?
- Is the system of three axes a description of reality, or a tool for organizing attention?
- When the practice becomes practical (overlaying life meanings), does it become more true—or more subjective?
