# 2.3.4 Multidimensional Meditation – Questions

## The Triangle as an Independent Figure — Deeper Questions

Use these questions to deepen the triangle practice and convert your observations into repeatable experiments.

---

### Core Inquiry

- What makes the triangle a complete form in your experience, rather than only a line plus one more point?
- In direct practice, what are your three points: possibility, movement, and quality of movement?

---

### Possibility, Probability, and Quality

- If the point is possibility and movement is probability, what exactly does the third factor change?
- Does changing the quality of movement alter the outcome, the meaning of the outcome, or both?
- Which is easier for you to control: direction of movement or quality of movement?

---

### Experiments

- Center scan: Do three short sessions emphasizing one center at a time (physical, emotional, intellectual). Which creates the most stable triangle?
- Mode test: Practice one session in pure flow, one at the boundary, and one in the emptiness mode. What differs in clarity, emotion, and after-effect?
- Transition threshold: Increase speed of switching point -> line -> triangle until coherence drops. Where is your threshold?
- Dual-direction test: Hold one movement axis while adding a second subtle direction. Does triangle perception become clearer or chaotic?

---

### Identity and Attention

- When you concentrate on the triangle, where is the "I" located: in one point, on the connecting line, or in the whole structure?
- Can you keep observer, object, and movement in awareness simultaneously?
- Does sustained triangle practice reduce identification with passing thoughts more than point-only practice?

---

### Daily Integration

- Choose one life domain (health, relationships, work, learning) and map it as a triangle: possibility, movement direction, and quality factor.
- Which one change would most improve your next outcome?

- In a difficult conversation, can you notice when your movement shifts between emotional and intellectual centers? What triggered the shift?

---

### Philosophical Prompts

- Is the triangle discovered in consciousness, or constructed by attention as a useful model?
- Are the three centers objective structures or practical lenses for self-regulation?
- If one point of the triangle is unstable, is the whole structure still valid as a guide for action?
